Sable Habitat

Sables thrive in forested areas with abundant small prey and seasonal fruits.

Seal Habitat

Seals inhabit marine environments, often hauling out on rocks, beaches, or ice.

Featured Animals

Goldfish

Goldfish are often thought of as being forgetful, a belief that has become part of popular culture. Their scientific name is Carassius auratus, though many people found it difficult to pronounce. For this reason, the simple yet familiar term “goldfish” was used.

Penguin

Penguins are flightless birds that are excellent swimmers, using their flippers to move quickly through the water. They are found mainly in the Southern Hemisphere, with the Emperor Penguin being the largest species.

Dragon

Dragons are mythical creatures that appear in stories and folklore around the world. In many cultures, they are symbols of power, wisdom, or danger, making them one of the most iconic legendary animals.
